# Beta Risk Register

> Risks for the Controlled Beta cohort (scope: [CONTROLLED_BETA_LAUNCH_PLAN.md](CONTROLLED_BETA_LAUNCH_PLAN.md)). Each risk has a likelihood × impact rating, the mitigation already in place, an early-warning signal (wired into the [Monitoring Plan](../runbooks/beta-first-30-days-monitoring.md)), a contingency, and an owner. **Sacred invariants (CLAUDE.md §2) are not "risks" to be traded off — they are guaranteed; the risks below are about detection, scale, and the deliberately-deferred launch-hardening items.**

Rating key — Likelihood: Low / Med / High · Impact: Low / Med / High / **Critical**. Priority = the combination that decides attention.

| # | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation (in place) | Early warning (monitor) | Contingency | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | **Cross-tenant data leak** (RLS regression) | Low | **Critical** | FORCE-RLS on all tenant tables; non-bypass `app_user`; `migrate:validate` asserts coverage in CI; restore drill re-checks; per-request `X-Company-Id` re-authorization | Onboarding isolation test (Ops §2); any cross-tenant row in spot checks | Freeze tenant → revoke surface (Ops §8) → RCA → block deploy | Platform lead |
| R2 | **Audit chain break** (tamper/corruption) | Low | **Critical** | Hash-chained append-only audit; `app.verify_audit_chain`; `GET /audit/verify` | **Daily** audit-chain verify across cohort (D5) | Freeze writes for tenant → restore from verified backup → RCA | Platform lead |

## Top watch-list for week 1
**R5** (install backup cron — blocks invite), **R14** (assign support owner — blocks invite), **R2/R3** (daily audit + balance checks), **R7** (OCR correction-rate baseline), **R8** (manual monitoring discipline). R5 and R14 are the only items that gate the **first invite**; the rest are operate-and-watch.
